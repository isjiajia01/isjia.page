Span · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Span does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Span has no server — nothing is sent anywhere.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, Span reads your calendars and reminders on your device in order to show your plans. Access is read-only: Span never creates, edits or deletes events or reminders. Your data never leaves your device.

Sample day

Before you grant Calendar and Reminders access, Span shows a clearly labelled sample day that exists only in memory for the life of the process. It is never written to your calendars, never mixed into your real plans, and disappears when the app closes.

Location

If you allow location while using the app, Span uses it only to estimate travel time to an outing you added. Location is optional, is not stored beyond the current session's use, and never leaves the device.

Purchases

Span has no in-app purchase and no subscription. There is no purchase data for the app to hold.
