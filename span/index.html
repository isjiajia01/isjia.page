Span

Plan the day you actually have.

Get Span See what it does

What it does

Your calendars, as one day.

Built on your calendars

Uses the Apple Calendar and Reminders you already keep.

Read-only

Span looks; it never edits or deletes your events or reminders.

Capacity in hours

Day capacity, with plans pencilled into the space that is actually left.

Explicit reflow

Shows the exact before and after before it moves anything.

One undo

Running-plan handoff, cross-day rollover recovery and a single Undo for each change.

No server

No sync of its own — everything stays on your device.

Before you grant access

The sample day, then your own.

01

A clearly labelled sample day

Until you allow Calendar and Reminders access, Span shows a sample day, so you can read the interface without exposing anything.

02

You decide

Grant access and your own day appears exactly as it is; the sample never mixes into it, and Span does not import it.

03

Span looks, it does not edit

Calendars and reminders are never modified. Plan durations, pencil slots and capacity are Span's own local data.

Span sits on top of the Calendar and Reminders you already keep. It helps you see your day and week as one picture — what's fixed, what's flexible, and where the day needs to give a little — without asking you to move your life into yet another app.

Travel time

Location, if you allow it, is used only to estimate travel time to an outing you added.

Price

Span is a free download with no subscription and no in-app purchase. There is no account to create and no data leaves the device.
