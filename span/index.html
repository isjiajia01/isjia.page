Span Planning

Plan the day you actually have.

Span is a planning layer that sits on top of the Calendar and Reminders you already keep. It helps you see your day and week as one picture — what's fixed, what's flexible, and where the day needs to give a little — without asking you to move your life into yet another app.

  • Built on your existing Apple Calendar and Reminders
  • Read-only: Span looks, it never edits or deletes your events
  • No server and no sync of its own — everything stays on your device
  • iOS 17 or later
