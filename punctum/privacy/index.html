Punctum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Punctum does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing is sent to a server operated by us.

Weather

If you choose the Weather source, Punctum asks Apple WeatherKit for conditions at your approximate location. Your location is used on your device only for this request and is not stored by Punctum.

Calendar

If you choose the Next event source, Punctum reads upcoming events on your device in order to show them. Event data never leaves your device.

Media

If you choose the Now Playing source, Punctum reads the current track title, artist and playback position on your device in order to show them. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Punctum schedules a local notification when a timer ends. It is created and delivered on your device.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Punctum only learns whether Punctum Pro is active; it receives no payment details.

Settings and sync

Your preferences are stored on your device and shared between Punctum on your iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple TV through Apple's own on-device mechanisms.
