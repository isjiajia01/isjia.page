Punctum — Dot-Matrix Clock

A clock with a pulse.

Punctum is a dot-matrix clock for iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple TV. Beyond the time, it can surface the things you glance at all day — the weather, your next calendar event, and what's playing — all rendered in dots.

  • Dot-matrix time and date displays, made to be read across the room
  • Optional sources: weather, next event, now playing, timers
  • Runs on iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple TV
