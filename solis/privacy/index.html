Solis · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Solis does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sent to a server operated by the developer.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Location

If you grant location permission, Solis uses your location on your device to calculate sun and moon times for where you are. Your location is never stored by Solis and never leaves your device. You can also pick a place manually instead.

Camera and Sky AR

Sky AR uses the camera only while it is open, and only to draw the Sun and Moon over what the camera sees. No photo or video is captured, stored or transmitted. Location and heading are used on the device for the same calculation.

Widgets, Apple Watch and Apple TV

Widgets read the place you selected from the app's own local storage. On Apple Watch and Apple TV, settings and your purchase are shared with your other devices through Apple's own on-device mechanisms. Solis does not run a sync service.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Solis receives no payment details.
