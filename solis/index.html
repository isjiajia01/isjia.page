Solis — Sun & Moon

The daylight, at a glance.

Solis tells you what the sky is doing today: sunrise and sunset, the golden hours photographers plan around, twilight, and the moon — its phase, rise and set. Open it, get the answer, put the phone away.

  • Sunrise, sunset, solar noon and twilight times
  • Golden hour and blue hour, morning and evening
  • Moon phase, moonrise and moonset
  • Works for your current location or anywhere you search
