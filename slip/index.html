What it does

A ledger that stays on your phone.

Today's allowance

Set a monthly budget and see the day's remaining allowance, with everyday spending kept separate from fixed costs like rent and subscriptions.

Quick entry

Amount and merchant, or one short sentence. Siri and Shortcuts can pass a transaction in; there is no bank connection.

Receipts, on device

Scan a paper receipt or pick a photo; text recognition runs on the iPhone, and you correct the fields before anything is saved.

Categories you control

Merchant matching and custom rules do the routine work; uncertain entries wait in a review queue for your decision.

Twelve currencies

DKK, EUR, USD, GBP, SEK, NOK, CNY, JPY, CHF, PLN, ISK and HKD, keeping the original amount with a conversion in your main currency.

Portable records

Summaries by category and period, plus JSON backup export and restore, free.

The boundaries

What Slip does, and what it does not.

It does

  • Keep the ledger and the receipt processing on your iPhone
  • Show today's remaining allowance against the budget you set
  • Read receipts on the device, with fields you check before anything is saved
  • Export and restore JSON backups, free

It does not

  • Connect to your bank
  • Read other apps' notifications
  • Upload your ledger anywhere
  • Keep a cloud copy of its own — a backup goes only where you send it

Slip is a personal expense ledger that keeps your records on your iPhone. Log everyday purchases, scan receipts, and see your spending in the context of the budget you actually set — not a category pie chart you never asked for.

Backups go where you send them

There is no cloud sync of its own: a backup you export goes wherever you send it, and that destination is your choice and your responsibility.

Free and Pro

Slip is a free download. Slip Pro is optional, offered as a yearly subscription ($11.99/year with a 7-day free introductory offer) or a one-time lifetime purchase ($19.99). Existing entries, manual logging, JSON backup and restore stay free either way. Restore Purchases lives on the paywall and under Settings → Slip Pro.
