What it changes

It changes rendering, not your history.

It defers

  • Work for conversation turns that are far offscreen, using Safari's own content-visibility support
  • Only those turns — the extension has no other job, and makes no network requests
  • Reversibly: Keep awake is Auto, 20, 40 or 80 recent messages, and Wake all reverses it

It never touches

  • Your prompts, code blocks and responses — nothing is deleted, rewritten or uploaded
  • Active, focused, selected and streaming turns
  • Cmd+F, copy and VoiceOver — designed to be preserved, though not independently verified here

Long ChatGPT and Gemini threads make Safari work harder and harder — fans spinning, scrolling stuttering, typing lagging. ThreadLite asks Safari to defer work for conversation turns that are far offscreen, so the page you are actually looking at stays responsive.
