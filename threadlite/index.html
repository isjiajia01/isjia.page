ThreadLite for Safari

Keep long AI chats responsive.

Long ChatGPT and Gemini threads make Safari work harder and harder — fans spinning, scrolling stuttering, typing lagging. ThreadLite trims the weight of those pages by cutting active DOM nodes by up to 87%, so marathon threads stay smooth.

  • Makes very long AI chat threads scroll and type smoothly again
  • Works with ChatGPT and Gemini in Safari on macOS
  • 100% local — everything happens in your browser, on your Mac
  • Zero tracking, zero analytics, nothing to sign up for
