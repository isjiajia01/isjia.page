A higher bar for everyday software.

Five rules that decide what ships and what gets cut.

01

Quiet

No badges begging for attention, no streaks, no notifications you did not ask for.

02

Local first

Data stays on your device. Where a network is needed, you bring the key and the server is yours.

03

One screen deep

Open it, get the answer, put the phone away. Depth is a last resort, not a feature.

04

Read from across the room

Big clocks, clear numbers, honest contrast — designed for a glance, not for a screenshot.

05

Buy it once

One-time purchases wherever the App Store allows it, and Family Sharing on by default.

06

Small

Single purpose by design. If a feature does not serve the one thing, it does not ship.
