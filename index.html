What I won't do.

A few rules I use to decide what gets cut.

No nagging

No badges, no streaks, no notifications you did not ask for. Where a network is needed, each app's privacy policy says what is sent.

One screen deep

Open it, get the answer, put the phone away.

Buy it once

One-time purchases wherever the App Store allows it, and Family Sharing on by default.

Small

If a feature does not serve the one thing, it does not ship.
