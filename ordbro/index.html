What it does

Read and watch in your own language.

Pages, on your terms

Translate the whole page beside the original or in place; select a few words to translate just those.

Video subtitles

Ordbro finds the subtitle file, translates ahead of the video and shows it in a clean layer that follows pause, seeking, speed and fullscreen.

Context aware

Names, pronouns and meaning stay consistent from line to line instead of being translated sentence by isolated sentence.

Your service, your key

OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a machine-translation service such as DeepL, Google Cloud Translation or Azure Translator.

Per-site rules

Translate on request, always, or never, per site. Login and payment pages are never cached.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no Ordbro server in the middle.

Three reading modes

Read it your way, page by page.

01

Translation only

The page in your language, with the original out of the way.

02

Bilingual

Translation alongside the original, paragraph by paragraph.

03

Original

Restores the page exactly as it was, and pauses translation requests.

Ordbro (Danish for “word bridge”) carries what you read and watch into your own language, whatever language it started in. Translate a page next to the original or replace it in place, or let Ordbro find the subtitles of a video and translate them ahead of playback.

Reading and watching

Web page translation is free. Video subtitle translation is part of Ordbro Pro, a one-time purchase with Family Sharing — no subscription. Translations are cached on your Mac, so reading or watching the same thing again costs nothing.

Subtitle translation needs a video that actually has subtitles. DRTV is available in Denmark and requires a free DR login to play; Ordbro neither changes nor bypasses that.

What you need

macOS with Safari, a translation provider and that provider's API key. There is nothing to sign up for in Ordbro itself: you paste the key once in Settings, press Test connection, and the provider bills you directly.

Ordbro is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by DR (Danmarks Radio).

Price

Ordbro is a free download. Ordbro Pro is a $3.99 one-time purchase with Family Sharing — no subscription.
