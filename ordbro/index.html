What it does

Two things, carried across.To ting, båret over.

Ordbro is Danish for “word bridge”. It takes the page you are reading, or the video you are watching, into your own language, whatever language it started in.

Web pages · free

The whole page, visible text first.

Translate a page beside the original or in place. What is on screen is translated first, the rest as you scroll. Select a few words to translate just those, or look them up, break a sentence down, or send it to Anki.

  • On request
  • Always translate
  • Never translate

Video subtitles · Ordbro Pro

Press play. The subtitles follow.

Ordbro finds a video's subtitle file, translates it ahead of playback and shows it in a layer of its own, in step with pause, seeking, speed and fullscreen. The captions are styled after Apple TV and sized to the video, not the page.

Privacy

Nothing in the middle.Intet imellem.

Goes to the provider you chose

  • The text you ask it to translate
  • Subtitle lines in batches, with a few lines of context
  • The page or programme title, to keep the translation consistent

Never happens

  • An Ordbro server, an account, analytics, ads or tracking
  • Your key going anywhere but your provider
  • Login and payment pages being translated or cached

Price

Ordbro is a free download, and web page translation is free. Video subtitle translation is part of Ordbro Pro, a $3.99 one-time purchase with Family Sharing — no subscription. Translations are cached on your Mac, so reading or watching the same thing again costs nothing.

What you need

macOS with Safari, a translation provider and that provider's API key. There is nothing to sign up for in Ordbro itself.

Subtitle translation needs a video that actually has subtitles. DRTV is available in Denmark and requires a free DR login to play; Ordbro neither changes nor bypasses that.

Ordbro is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by DR (Danmarks Radio), YouTube, or any other site whose pages or subtitles it can translate.
